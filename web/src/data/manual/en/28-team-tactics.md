# Match: Team Tactics

Chapter 15 covers the three dimensions (tempo / pitch width / defensive line). This chapter covers **team tactics** — a separate choice: **pick 1 of 5, or pick none.**

Team tactics and the three dimensions are two different layers, and they stack:

- The **three dimensions** are knobs. Each notch moves things a little, and you can combine them freely.
- A **team tactic** is a route. It pushes the whole team's play in one direction, and the price is that you must field **4 specific position slots**.

---

## 1. The five tactics (quick reference)

| Tactic | The 4 slots it needs | Where it pushes play | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Possession** | Midfield ×3 + Central AM **or** Wide ×1 | Short passing, almost no crosses, almost no long shots | Keep the ball on the ground |
| **High Press** | Central AM **or** Wide ×2 + CF ×1 + CB ×1 | Crosses, through balls and dribbles all present; pressing much harder | Push the game into their half |
| **Counter** | CB ×2 + Full-back ×2 | Through balls and dribbles, the fewest attacking moves of all five | Give up the ball, wait for the opening |
| **Central** | DM ×1 + Midfield ×2 + Central AM ×1 | The most through balls, almost no crosses | Break through the middle |
| **Wings** | Wide ×2 + Full-back ×2 | The most crosses, the fewest through balls | Stretch them to the flanks |

> **Not choosing is also a choice.** No team tactic means the engine's balanced default. If you cannot field the 4 slots a tactic needs, that is the only option (see section 2).

---

## 2. The gate is hard

**A tactic needs 4 of your 11 on-pitch slots to fall inside the specified families.** It is not "more is better" — it is **all-or-nothing**. Meet it and the tactic applies; miss it and you cannot pick the tactic at all.

Families are counted by **slot**, not by the player's natural position:

| Family | Slots it contains |
|---|---|
| Centre-back | CBL / CB / CBR |
| Full-back | LB / RB / LWB / RWB |
| Defensive midfield | DMFL / DMF / DMFR |
| Midfield | CML / CM / CMR |
| Central attacking mid | CAML / CAM / CAMR |
| Wide | LM / RM / LW / RW |
| Centre-forward | CFL / CF / CFR |

**The traps:**

- One slot can only satisfy one requirement. The 4 requirements must sit on 4 different slots.
- The goalkeeper never counts toward any requirement.
- **LM / RM count the same as LW / RW.** Possession and Wings both accept LM / RM.
- **"Central AM or Wide" is a union.** The 2 slots High Press needs can both be AM, both be wide, or one of each.
- You field a **slot**, not a player. Move a CM out to LW and that slot counts as Wide, not Midfield.

**What if you cannot field it?** One answer: choose no tactic. The game will not quietly downgrade the tactic, and partial requirements do nothing.

---

## 3. The five tactics, one by one

### Possession

- **Needs:** Midfield ×3 + (Central AM or Wide) ×1
- **How it plays:** nearly everything goes through **short passing**. Crosses are squeezed to a minimum and long shots almost disappear.
- **In the match:** slightly fewer fouls than usual — your players are on the ball, not chasing it.
- **Good fit:** midfield-heavy shapes (4-3-3 / 4-5-1 / 3-5-2), especially when you can name three midfielders.
- **The cost:** one-dimensional. If your short-passing route cannot get through their middle, you have no second route.

### High Press

- **Needs:** (Central AM or Wide) ×2 + CF ×1 + CB ×1
- **How it plays:** noticeably more fouls; the whole team pushes up a notch, so attack and possession are both slightly better and the press wins the ball more often.
- **In the match:** this is "trade fouls for the ball". You will break up play more often than your opponent does.
- **Good fit:** mobile front players plus one centre-back who can step out (4-2-3-1 / 4-3-3).
- **The cost:** more fouls means more free kicks conceded and more cards. On average it is also **slightly worse than no tactic at all** — the fouls cost more than the press buys back. You pick it for the way it plays, not because it is stronger.

### Counter

- **Needs:** CB ×2 + Full-back ×2 — a complete back four.
- **How it plays:** the **most through balls and dribbles** of any tactic. Possession is given up on purpose, and it produces the **fewest attacking moves** of the five.
- **In the match:** you do not need the ball. Win it, then go forward in one or two passes — and a team that sits deep also **intercepts noticeably more**.
- **Good fit:** five-at-the-back or a back four with a double pivot; against opponents who dominate the ball and push high.
- **The cost:** few moves means **low margin for error**. The chances are rare, and missing them costs you.

### Central

- **Needs:** DM ×1 + Midfield ×2 + Central AM ×1
- **How it plays:** **almost no crosses**; the most through balls of the five. Everything funnels into the middle.
- **In the match:** the same direction as a narrow pitch width, but more extreme.
- **Good fit:** shapes that stack the middle (4-1-2-1-2, two-striker 3-5-2) with midfielders who can break forward.
- **The cost:** the flanks are essentially abandoned. Against a packed middle you have no Plan B.

### Wings

- **Needs:** Wide ×2 + Full-back ×2
- **How it plays:** the **most crosses** of the five and the fewest through balls. Attacks are pushed to the touchlines.
- **In the match:** the same direction as a wide pitch setting.
- **Good fit:** real wingers plus attacking full-backs (4-4-2 / a wide 4-2-3-1).
- **The cost:** crossing is one of the lower-percentage ways to attack. You will get plenty of crosses without necessarily getting goals.

---

## 4. How it combines with the three dimensions

The two layers **multiply**; they are not alternatives:

- **Same direction** → stronger. Wings + wide pitch + fast tempo all push play to the flanks and up the pace.
- **Opposite directions** → they cancel. Wings + narrow pitch means the tactic pulls wide while the width setting pulls central.
- **No conflict** → they simply coexist. Defensive line does not collide with any of the five.

Practical pairings:

| The picture you want | Team tactic | Three dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| Control it, no mistakes | Possession | Slow / Narrow / Mid |
| Swarm them | High Press | Fast / Balanced / High |
| Underdog vs favourite | Counter | Fast / Balanced / Low |
| Force it through the middle | Central | Balanced / Narrow / High |
| Dominate the flanks | Wings | Balanced / Wide / Mid |

---

## 5. Are the five equally strong?

**No — but the gap is very small.**

In testing, none of the five is reliably more than 2 percentage points better than picking no tactic. Possession is the only one that comes out slightly ahead; High Press comes out slightly behind; the other three are level. That gap is **far smaller** than what squad, player quality and the three dimensions are worth.

So pick a tactic because of the way you want to play, not because one is stronger. The real cost is the one from section 2: **you pay 4 slots for it.**

---

## 6. Common mistakes

- **Treating the gate as "more is better".** It is not. Exactly 4 slots; extra slots in the same family buy nothing.
- **Expecting a downgrade.** If the gate fails, the tactic is unavailable. There is no half-strength version.
- **Counting by the player's natural position.** The gate reads the **slot**. A CM pushed out to LW occupies a wide slot.
- **Forgetting LM / RM are Wide.** A squad with only wingers can still field Wings.
- **Treating High Press as an attacking tactic.** It buys pressing and ball-winning and pays in fouls. The attack is only marginally better, while the fouls cost more.
- **Changing the lineup after picking a tactic.** A reshuffle can break up the 4 slots and the tactic then cannot be deployed. Re-check after any change.

---

## 7. How it relates to everything else

- **Tempo / width / line:** they stack — see section 4.
- **Substitutions:** a team tactic applies to the **whole team**, so substitutions do not break it. But **moving a player can break the gate** — shift your only central midfielder out wide and the requirement fails.
- **Player quality:** the gate does not care how good your players are, only whether the **slots** are right. But the effect itself is **team-wide** — having a particularly good winger does not earn you extra in Wings.
- **Specialties:** independent systems. Specialties attach to players; a team tactic attaches to the team.
