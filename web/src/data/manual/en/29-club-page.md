# Club Pages and Visitors

Club names in the standings, the squad-status board and the transfer market are all
clickable. Opening one takes you to that club's **club page** — its public profile.

---

## 1. What the page shows

| Area | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Crest, name, short code, league, city, founding year, league position |
| Club details | Manager, stadium, supporters |
| Season record | Wins / draws / losses, goals for, against, difference, points |
| Recent match | The last opponent and the score |
| Recent visitors | Who has looked at this club |

The manager line links to their **manager profile**; the stadium links to that ground's page.

---

## 2. Why there is no squad list

The club page deliberately shows **no player list**. Another club's player attributes are
not public information — only players listed on the transfer market can be inspected.

The only squad you can browse is your own.

---

## 3. Manager profiles

A manager profile is public. It shows:

- the username
- a bio, if the manager wrote one
- the join date
- supporter level

Private settings such as email, timezone and language never appear there.

The bio is edited under **Settings → Profile** and takes effect immediately.

---

## 4. Recent visitors

The bottom right of a club page has a **Recent visitors** panel:

- today's visitor count in the corner
- up to eight visitors, most recent first
- click a name to open that manager's profile

The same panel appears on **league pages**.

---

## 5. How visit records work

> Visitor records are kept for three days and then removed automatically.

A few rules worth knowing:

- **Only signed-in users are recorded.** Browsing while logged out leaves no trace and is
  not counted.
- **Looking at your own club does not count.** Otherwise every manager would sit at the top
  of their own list forever.
- **Repeated visits occupy one slot**, showing the most recent time.
- **The panel can never break the page.** If visitor data cannot be read, the panel is
  simply empty and everything else renders as usual.

---

## 6. Common misunderstandings

❌ **"The club page is my dashboard."**
No. The dashboard is private and is where you manage things; the club page is the public
face that other managers see.

❌ **"Visit history is kept forever."**
Three days only. It answers "who has been paying attention lately", not "who ever came".

❌ **"Clicking a club name switches me to that club."**
It does not. It opens that club's page; nothing about your own club changes.
